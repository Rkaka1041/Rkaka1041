## ☁️ About Me

> *"Build infrastructure that doesn't wake you up at 3am."*

I'm a **DevSecOps Engineer & Site Reliability Engineer** based in 📍 **Chicago, Illinois**, with **6+ years of experience** keeping large-scale cloud platforms secure, stable, and fast. I've worked across **TransUnion**, **Dollar General**, and **P&G** — some of the most demanding production environments in the country.

I believe **security shouldn't slow teams down** — I embed DevSecOps controls directly into delivery pipelines so that shipping fast and shipping safe aren't a trade-off.

Beyond engineering, I actively publish **peer-reviewed research** at the intersection of AI and cloud security — 5 published papers covering AI-driven compliance, multi-cloud governance, and predictive security systems.

- 🔒 I've never met a Kubernetes cluster I couldn't secure
- 📡 On-call doesn't scare me — I *am* the runbook
- 🤖 I publish AI + cloud security research in my spare time
- 🌎 Built cloud infra across AWS, Azure & GCP — on 2 continents
